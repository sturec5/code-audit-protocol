# Code of Conduct

This is a small project with one maintainer. That is exactly why this file is
short and specific rather than a governance structure that does not exist.

## What is expected

Argue with the work. Disagreement about whether a phase earns its place, whether
an invariant is provable, or whether a finding holds up is the point of this
repository — a review culture that cannot say "you are wrong, here is the
evidence" is not a review culture.

Argue with the work, not the person. Those are different, and everyone can tell
which one is happening.

Assume the other person read the thing carefully and reached a different
conclusion. Ask before concluding otherwise.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, in the ordinary sense of the word: personal attacks, demeaning or
discriminatory comments, unwanted attention after being asked to stop, publishing
someone's private information, or sustained disruption of a discussion.

Also, specific to what this repository does: do not open an issue or pull request
containing confidential material — real incident reports, internal system names,
customer data, credentials. `CONTRIBUTING.md` asks for invented examples for this
reason. If you paste something you should not have, say so and it will be removed
from the history rather than merely deleted.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and anywhere someone is
representing this project.

## Reporting

Email **sturec@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer and nobody else.

Say what happened and link it if it is public. You do not need to prove
anything or decide in advance whether it "counts" — that is the maintainer's
job, not yours.

If the report is about the maintainer, you are entitled to say so publicly
instead. A reporting channel that routes complaints about the only person with
authority back to that same person is not a real channel, and pretending
otherwise would be worse than admitting the limit.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides, and will say what was decided and why — briefly, without
naming the reporter. Responses range from a private request to stop, through
edited or removed content, to a block.

Contributions are judged on their merits regardless of who wrote them. Nothing
here is a reason to reject a technically sound contribution, and nothing here
makes a bad one acceptable.

## Attribution

The expectations above are the ordinary ones, in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org). It is not
reproduced here, because a document nobody reads protects nobody.
